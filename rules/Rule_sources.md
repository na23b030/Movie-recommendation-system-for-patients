# Rule Sources
- **S01 — [Epilepsy Foundation: Photosensitivity](https://www.epilepsy.com/what-is-epilepsy/seizure-triggers/photosensitivity)** · R-01, R-02, R-13. Flashing/patterned light can trigger seizures in susceptible people. Excluding unknown or low-confidence “no flashing” reports is **our precautionary policy**.
- **S02 — [MPA Ratings Guide](https://www.filmratings.com/ratings-guide/)** · R-03, R-04, R-07. Explains NC-17, R (adult accompaniment permitted), and PG-13. Our stricter R exclusion and PG-13 score penalty are **inferred**.
- **S03 — [BBFC Age Ratings](https://www.bbfc.co.uk/our-age-ratings)** · R-03, R-05, R-06. Describes 15/18 age limits and 12 versus 12A. Treating 12A as an unconditional under-12 exclusion is **our conservative choice**.
- **S04 — [CBFC Certification](https://cbfcindia.gov.in/cbfcAdmin/certification.php)** · R-03, R-08. Describes Indian certificates including U, UA, and A. Penalizing legacy UA for under-12 viewers is **our preference heuristic**, not a prohibition.
- **S05 — [VA: Trauma Triggers](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/understand/what/trauma_triggers.asp)** · R-09, R-14. Media may act as distress reminders for some people. Respecting explicit avoidances and penalizing estimated threat are **individualized design choices**, not universal diagnoses.
- **S06 — [NCI: Fatigue](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/side-effects/fatigue)** · R-10. Fatigue can affect energy and attention. A **120-minute** movie cutoff and **−0.17** penalty are inferred.
- **S07 — [NCI: Memory and Concentration](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/side-effects/memory)** · R-11. Some patients experience concentration difficulties. Penalizing a curator-estimated dense plot is **inferred**.
- **S08 — [NHS: Migraine](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/migraine/)** · R-12, R-13. Migraine can involve nausea and light sensitivity. The vomiting-content and flashing penalties are **inferred**, not NHS film guidelines.
- **S09 — [W3C: Captions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/)** · R-15. Explains caption access. Requiring verified caption availability is **our eligibility policy**.
- **S10 — [W3C: Audio Description](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/description/)** · R-16. Explains access to visual information. Requiring verified audio description is **our eligibility policy**.
- **S11 — [NCI: Treatment Side Effects](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/side-effects)** · R-10, R-12. Symptoms vary between people; **never infer symptoms from diagnosis or treatment alone**.
